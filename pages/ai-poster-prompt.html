Transform your normal photos into trending Instagram posters with the power of AI. One click is all it takes.

Do you want to turn your ordinary photos into stunning Instagram posters? With this AI photo editing prompt, you can create professional poster-style edits in seconds. No design skills or expensive software required.

The AI Poster Prompt

"Transform this photo into an Instagram poster edit. Add bold typography with the text '{your caption}' at the bottom. Apply a cinematic color grade with warm tones, add a soft vignette, and create a blurred background effect around the subject. Make it look like a professional movie poster."

How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open ChatGPT, Gemini, or your preferred AI image editor.
  2. Upload the photo you want to edit.
  3. Copy and paste the poster prompt above.
  4. Replace "{your caption}" with your desired text.
  5. Wait for the AI to generate your poster edit.
  6. Download and share on Instagram.

This prompt works with ChatGPT 4, Google Gemini, and most AI image editing tools. The results are perfect for Instagram posts, stories, and reels.

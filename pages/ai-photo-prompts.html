Best AI Photo Editing Prompts

Prompt 1: Clean Studio Background

"Remove the background from this photo and replace it with a clean white studio background. Apply soft studio lighting to the subject."

Prompt 2: Cinematic Portrait

"Transform this photo into a cinematic portrait with warm golden hour lighting, shallow depth of field, and film grain effect."

"Apply a soft pastel color grading with increased saturation in the sky and skin tones. Add a subtle glow effect."

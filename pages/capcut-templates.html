How to Use CapCut Templates

  1. Download CapCut from your app store.
  2. Tap on "Templates" at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Search for the template name or paste the template link.
  4. Tap "Use Template" and select your photos or video clips.
  5. Export and share on Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube.
